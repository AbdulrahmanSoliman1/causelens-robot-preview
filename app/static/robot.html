          How reliable is this? The robot names the parts of each scene itself (no fixed list of concepts). On 18 fresh scenes it had never seen, half with problems outside any list we wrote (a flat tyre, snow on a solar panel, a kinked hose), its "will it work?" call was right 30 of 36 times, and every mistake was a false alarm (it never said "works" when the action was blocked); asking Muse Spark directly got 26, our earlier fixed-vocabulary version 22 (small sample). The picture follows the call: plain Muse Image got 1 of 25 blocked scenes right, CauseLens draws the call right 48 of 50 times. Calls can still be wrong, which is why it shows its reasons and what it couldn't check, and a person approves before the robot acts.
